## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>What is SearchAble?</b></summary>
<br>

SearchAble is file search software for Windows, from LifeAi. It searches inside your files by words and by meaning, across documents, photos, recordings and videos, and answers questions from your files with a source for every fact. It all runs on your PC, so it works offline too.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I search for text inside files on Windows?</b></summary>
<br>

Choose your folders in SearchAble, and it reads the content of every file in the background, including PDF and Word files, text inside pictures and speech in recordings. Then type a word, a sentence or a description, and you get the files with the matching sentence highlighted.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How is SearchAble different from Windows Search?</b></summary>
<br>

Windows Search mostly finds files by name and exact words. SearchAble also understands meaning: describe it in other words, and it finds the sentence in a document, what's in a photo or the moment in a recording.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is there file search software that works offline?</b></summary>
<br>

Yes. In SearchAble the scanning, the search and the answers all run on your PC. You only need an internet connection to download the models and check for updates.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I search photos by description?</b></summary>
<br>

Yes. Type what's in the picture, like “dog playing in the snow”, and SearchAble finds the photos and marks what matched. You can also drop a picture on the window to find similar ones.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I search what was said in a recording or video?</b></summary>
<br>

Yes. With transcription on, recordings and videos are transcribed in the background, and a search result jumps to the moment it was said.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Are my files sent over the internet?</b></summary>
<br>

No. Your files, the index and your chats stay on your PC, and the AI runs on it. The full details are in the <a href="https://lifeai.ovh/en/privacy">privacy policy</a>.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need a powerful graphics card?</b></summary>
<br>

No. SearchAble uses any graphics card with Direct3D 12, built-in graphics included, and runs on the processor without one. With an NVIDIA card, transcription is especially fast.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does SearchAble work on Mac?</b></summary>
<br>

For now, SearchAble is available only for Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) and Windows 11.

</details>
